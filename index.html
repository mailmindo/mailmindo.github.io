Privacy Policy for MailMindo

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

MailMindo ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

MailMindo operates directly within your browser side panel to assist with email productivity:

  • Email Content: When explicitly triggered by clicking "Summarize" or "Draft Response", MailMindo reads the text content of the active email thread on your screen to generate draft responses or summaries.
  • Account Information: We store basic authentication details (email address and login session tokens) managed securely via Supabase.
  • User Preferences: Custom voice engine profile samples and tone preferences are stored locally in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide and improve MailMindo's core functionality:

  • To analyze thread context and generate AI-powered draft responses and summaries.
  • To match your writing cadence and voice style based on user-provided samples.
  • To manage your account login session and track basic usage metrics (e.g., estimated time saved).

3. Data Protection & Sharing

MailMindo does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or email content to third parties or advertising networks. Email text processed during AI requests is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS directly to AI processing endpoints strictly for generating immediate outputs and is not retained for model training.

4. Data Storage and Security

All sensitive user authentication sessions are handled securely via Supabase infrastructure. Local preferences stay strictly within your local Chrome profile storage.

5. Your Rights & Control

You have full control over your data. You can clear your stored voice profile samples at any time inside the extension settings or sign out to clear local storage session keys.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@mailmindo.com
