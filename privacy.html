Privacy Policy for MailMindo

Effective Date: October 2026

MailMindo ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

MailMindo processes email context from your active Gmail threads solely to provide AI-generated reply suggestions and summaries. We do not store, sell, or trade your personal email contents or personal identification details.

2. How We Use Information

Any data processed by MailMindo is used strictly to:

  • Generate context-aware email response drafts inside your Gmail sidepanel.
  • Provide one-click thread summaries.
  • Manage active user subscriptions via Paystack.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is processed securely. MailMindo does not permanently store your email contents on external servers. Authentication and subscription metadata are securely managed using Supabase and Paystack.

4. Third-Party Services

MailMindo utilizes trusted third-party service providers, including Paystack for payment processing and Supabase for authentication. These services handle data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@mailmindo.com.
